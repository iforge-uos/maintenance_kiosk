# Nozzle Clog SOP

## Symptoms
- Filament does not extrude
- Extrusion stops mid-print
- Extruder clicks or grinds filament

## Steps
- Heat nozzle to the loaded material temperature.
- Attempt a controlled purge.
- If purge fails, unload filament and inspect the filament tip.
- Perform a cold pull or nozzle clearing routine.
- Reload filament and confirm steady extrusion.

## Save Summary
Use "Nozzle clog cleared" when extrusion is restored.
