Pulling the filament out of idler

V 20260226

References: Prusa writes quite detailed instruction sheets, so we can copy from there,

https://help.prusa3d.com/article/regular-printer-maintenance-core-one_829710

Tools and needed

  Plier

Preperation

Nozzle temperature is 200+

1. opens the idler

Open the idler by lifting the top lever up, then the mechanism will be opened by itself

2. Pull filament out

Once you spot the filament, you can pull it out with a pair of pliers, then continue pulling in a downward direction to prevent the print head from moving from its paused position.

3. Close the Filament idler

Lift bottom lever up

Put top lever down, with slight pulling to the left to lock its position

Test

Reload filament back in, like how you normally change filament, purge filament.

Done!!!
