Blob removal

Total duration ~ 40min

V 20260514

References: pictures copied from and 3DJake

https://help.prusa3d.com/product/core-one/printer-maintenance_247

https://www.printables.com/model/1259660-prusa-core-one-hotend-fan-spacer

Tools and needed

  1. Pliers
  2. 2.5mm allen key
  3. Brass brush

Preperation

Heat the nozzle temperature to 270 °C and wait for 15 minutes.

  1. Remove blob

1.1 Hold the blob and wiggle it around; it should come off. Be careful of HOT molten plastic!!

1.2 Clean the rest of the blob with pliers

2.1 Set the nozzle temperature to 90C and wait for it to cool down, (around 10 min)

2.2 use pliers to peel residuals off

2.3 Take off the silicon sock with pliers and peel off the leftover plastic (be careful not to break heat wires)

3.1

Clean the nozzle with brass brush

Assemble it back

  1. Put back silicon sock with pliers or hand when cooled down
  2. Tighten the fan screw with a 2.5mm Allen key, if it came loose due to the blob

       

Test

Purge filament to get all the wasted plastic out

Done !!!
