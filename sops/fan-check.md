# Fan Check SOP

## Symptoms
- Fan does not spin
- Fan noise changes suddenly
- Printer reports fan error

## Steps
- Power down if debris is visible near the fan.
- Remove loose filament strands or debris.
- Spin fan gently by hand to check obstruction.
- Restart printer and confirm fan movement.
- Escalate if the fan still fails to spin.

## Save Summary
Use "Fan debris cleared" when the fan operates normally.
