# Bed Adhesion SOP

## Symptoms
- First layer does not stick
- Corners lift during the first few layers
- Filament smears or beads on the bed

## Steps
- Clean the build plate.
- Check Z offset or first layer calibration.
- Confirm bed temperature and filament profile.
- Reapply glue when required.
- Run a small first-layer verification print.

## Save Summary
Use "Bed cleaned and re-leveled" when adhesion is restored.
